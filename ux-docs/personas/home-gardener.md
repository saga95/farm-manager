# 👤 Persona: Anjali, the Home Gardener (future tenant)

> _"I have an awkward strip behind the house and 180 ginger plants. I need to remember what I did and when, not count every plant."_

Role in the product: **OWNER of a separate tenant**. This persona is based on home-gardener and small-farmer feedback (SRS §41.2–§41.4). It is the reason the platform is multi-tenant and not coconut-only.

## 🎯 Goals

### Primary Goal

> Manage each batch of 100–300 plants as **one production cycle**: maintenance (watering, fertilizer, weeding) and several harvests, without numbering individual plants (AC-PC-001).

### Secondary Goals

- Record unused or awkward spaces (size, sun, water access) to decide what to grow (§41.2).
- See what each space grew before.
- Track harvests in kg and occasional sales to neighbours.

---

## 😤 Frustrations & Pain Points

- Farm apps assume large fields or individual plants.
- Forgets when fertilizer was last applied.
- Has no clear record of which space did well.

## 📖 Scenario

> Anjali creates the space "Back strip" (1.2 × 8 m, partial shade, near a tap) and starts "Ginger Batch 2026-01 – 180 plants" in it. Each weekend Anjali logs watering and fertilizer for the whole batch with one tap. Months later Anjali records three partial harvests (2.1 kg, 3.4 kg, 1.8 kg), and the space page shows what it produced.

---

## 🧭 Design implications

- The polytunnel, backyard and coconut areas all appear under **Farm** as zones and growing spaces. Coconut is not the only path.
- Logging an activity for a whole batch is a two-tap action.
- Harvest units are chosen per crop (count, kg or g).
- No coconut-specific language on generic crop screens.
